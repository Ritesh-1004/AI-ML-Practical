SMART APPLIANCE TROUBLESHOOTING SYSTEM
Sample Input and Output

========================================
TC1 - Power Supply or Fuse Problem
========================================

Input:

?- start.

Does the appliance fail to turn on? (y/n): y.
Is there no power indicator? (y/n): y.

Output:

-> RESULT: Power supply or fuse problem.


========================================
TC2 - Faulty Power Switch
========================================

Input:

?- start.

Does the appliance fail to turn on? (y/n): y.
Is there no power indicator? (y/n): n.
Does the appliance fail to turn on? (y/n): y.
Is the power switch not responding? (y/n): y.

Output:

-> RESULT: Faulty power switch.


========================================
TC3 - Motor Problem
========================================

Input:

?- start.

Does the appliance fail to turn on? (y/n): n.
Does the appliance fail to turn on? (y/n): n.
Does the appliance make unusual noise? (y/n): y.
Is the motor not running properly? (y/n): y.

Output:

-> RESULT: Motor problem.


========================================
TC4 - Unknown Problem
========================================

Input:

?- start.

Does the appliance fail to turn on? (y/n): n.
Does the appliance fail to turn on? (y/n): n.
Does the appliance make unusual noise? (y/n): n.

Output:

-> RESULT: Unable to determine the problem. Consult a technician.
